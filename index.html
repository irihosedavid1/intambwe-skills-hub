The Problem We Solve

Rwanda's Skills-to-Jobs Gap is Real

According to the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR, 2026), youth unemployment stood at 14.7% in 2025 — higher than the adult rate of 10.8%.

Labour underutilization among youth reached 56.7%. Many young Rwandans have skills but no simple, trusted way to prove them to local employers.

Intambwe Skills Hub is the bridge.
